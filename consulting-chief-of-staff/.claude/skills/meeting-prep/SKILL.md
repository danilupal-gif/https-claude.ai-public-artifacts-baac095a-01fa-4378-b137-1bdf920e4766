# Meeting prep

1. Identify the meeting from the calendar, or from the name Danielle gives.
2. Gather what's known: past emails, CRM or `memory/pipeline.md` entry, client file if one exists, and past call notes.
3. For a public business, you may look at their website if web access is available. Note what you saw and that it came from their site.
4. Write one page:
   - **Who and why:** person, business, how they found Danielle, what they want
   - **What we know:** tools, team size, pain points, numbers mentioned
   - **Fit check:** each fit criterion from the constitution: yes, no or unknown
   - **Goal for this call:** the one decision or next step to reach
   - **Questions to ask:** 5 at most, ending with budget, timing and who decides
   - **Offer to suggest:** usually the Business Workflow Review, and why
   - **For an existing client:** milestones, open items, what they owe, what we owe
5. Save to `briefings/prep-YYYY-MM-DD-<name>.md`.

Never put another client's details in a prep file. Refer to past results only as "a wellness client" unless written permission is recorded.
