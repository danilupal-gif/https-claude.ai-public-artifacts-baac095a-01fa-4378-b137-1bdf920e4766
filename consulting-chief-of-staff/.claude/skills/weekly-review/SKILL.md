# Weekly review

1. Read `constitution.md`, all of `memory/`, and this week's briefings.
2. Write:
   - **Clients:** each one, on track, at risk or late, with one line why
   - **Hours:** ask Danielle for hours per client this week; compare with available hours in the constitution
   - **Pipeline:** count by stage, what moved, what stalled over 14 days
   - **Proof captured:** baselines, results, testimonials and permissions gained this week
   - **Launch plan:** done, slipping, next
   - **Lessons:** anything to add to a reusable template (journey map, messages, checks, guides). Name the template; don't copy client data into it.
   - **Next week's top 3**
3. Save to `briefings/review-YYYY-MM-DD.md`.
