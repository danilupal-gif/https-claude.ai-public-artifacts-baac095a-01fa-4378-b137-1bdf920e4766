# Daily briefing

`constitution.md` sets the checks, format and voice. Follow it over anything here.

1. **Load context.** Read `constitution.md`, all of `memory/` (including `memory/clients/`), and the most recent briefing so you don't repeat what's handled. Mark carried-over items "still open".
2. **Gather (read only).** Last 24 hours, or since Friday on Mondays:
   - Gmail: unanswered emails from clients and prospects
   - Calendar: today and tomorrow
   - CRM or `memory/pipeline.md`: next steps due, new leads
   - Call notes from the last 2 days: promises Danielle made
   - Each client file: milestones, open items, what's waiting on the client
   - `memory/launch-plan.md`: items due this week
3. **Run the daily checks** from the constitution.
4. **Draft, never send:** replies to waiting clients, prospect follow-ups, and "promise made" emails. Create Gmail drafts if possible; otherwise add them under "Drafts" at the end of the briefing file. Client drafts follow the "protect the method" rule in `CLAUDE.md`.
5. **Pick the top 3.** Use the constitution's priority order. Client commitments come before sales, and sales before firm building.
6. **Write and save** to `briefings/YYYY-MM-DD.md`, in the constitution's format.
7. **Learn.** When interactive, ask: "Anything to stop flagging, or remember?" Save answers to `memory/`.
