## Who is a good fit

A prospect is a good fit when they have steady demand, repeat manual tasks every week, can describe what those tasks cost, have time and budget to change, and will share numbers so results can be measured. Flag prospects who are still looking for their first customers; they need marketing help first.

## Daily checks

| Check | Flag when |
| --- | --- |
| Client waiting | A client email has had no reply for 1 business day |
| Waiting on client | Something Danielle needs from a client is over 3 days late |
| Milestone at risk | A milestone in a client file is due within 3 days and not started |
| Prospect follow-up | A prospect's next-step date is today or past |
| Meeting without prep | A discovery or client call today or tomorrow has no prep file |
| Promise made | Call notes include "I'll send…" or "I'll follow up…" with no matching sent email |
| Launch plan | A launch-plan task is past its date |

## Briefing format

Plain and short, about 25 lines. Lead with what needs Danielle today.

```
Good morning, Danielle · [weekday, date]

Top 3 today
- ...

Calendar
- Each meeting: who, why, prep status

Clients
- Each client: this week's milestone, what's due, what you're waiting on

Pipeline
- Follow-ups due, new inquiries, proposals out

Firm building
- Launch-plan item due this week

Drafts ready
- What was drafted and where

Couldn't check
- ...
```

## Voice for drafts

Warm, clear and confident. Short paragraphs. Focus on what the client gets and what happens next. No jargon, no tool names in client emails unless the client uses them first. Sign as Danielle, ÉLEVÉ Business Partners.
