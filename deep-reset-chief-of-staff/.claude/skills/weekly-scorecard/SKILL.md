# Weekly scorecard

1. Read `constitution.md` and `memory/baseline.md`.
2. For the previous Monday–Sunday, gather from Stripe and HubSpot:
   - New leads, by source
   - Assessments started and finished (completion rate)
   - Purchases, by program, and revenue
   - Median time from payment to completed onboarding
   - Paid customers who needed manual onboarding help (HubSpot tasks tagged "manual onboarding")
   - Participants finished, paused and dropped, with recorded reasons
3. Ask Keon for one number you can't get from the tools: her admin hours that week.
4. Write a table: measure · baseline · this week · change. Under it, write at most 3 plain sentences on what improved, what slipped, and one suggestion.
5. Save it to `briefings/scorecard-YYYY-MM-DD.md`.

Never present revenue as profit. If cost-per-program figures are in `memory/baseline.md`, also show what each program earned after delivery costs.
